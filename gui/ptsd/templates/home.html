About the Framework

Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) is a complex psychiatric condition where early intervention significantly improves long-term clinical outcomes. This framework provides clinicians with a data-driven "second opinion" by evaluating patterns across 28 patient features simultaneously.


The model was trained using real-world data in Google Colab and integrates clinical assessments, lifestyle factors, and emotional indicators to provide a comprehensive, multi-dimensional risk assessment that goes beyond traditional screening tools.
